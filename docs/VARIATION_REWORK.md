# v0.4: staging and variation

Audience feedback on v0.3 identified repeated geometry and camera cycles despite
different chapter names. This revision changes the actual scenes. The music,
76 directed chapter boundaries and three-act length remain intact.

Each world's chronological occurrence selects a separate scene state without
wrapping back to the first four variants. Chapters use continuous progress and
one camera path instead of four automatic shots. The integrated motion clock
never rewinds at chapter or act boundaries and supports deterministic seeking.

Within 28–76% of each chapter, the strongest analyzed onset and bass rise select
one main accent. `event` is a short pulse there; `action` advances from zero to
one around it for irreversible opening, assembly or destruction. FFT bands
continue to drive smaller motion and lighting. This is deterministic analysis,
not a claim that numerical timing guarantees good musical direction.

Act I develops eight worlds through different structures and actions. Generic
swarm/spectrum overlays have been removed from the quieter and sculptural
worlds so their silhouettes remain readable. Act II is the hardware ray-tracing
section, with new mechanical, architectural and glass set pieces. Act III uses
OpenGL for broader changes in terrain, vegetation, gravity and the ending.

Bloom and radial blur have been reduced; strong accents receive short bursts
rather than constant blur. Antialiasing also covers Act I.

Native tour captures now sample 18% and 82% of each chapter. `tools/storyboard.py`
groups those images by world so repeated silhouettes are easy to spot. These
are inspection aids. Build checks, image differences and a technical score do
not establish entertainment quality or replace continuous viewing.

The v0.3 video, binaries and evidence are preserved. New verification and an
independent review must refer to the revised build explicitly.
