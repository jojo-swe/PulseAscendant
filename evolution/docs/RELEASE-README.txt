EVOLUTION
=========

A tribute to Second Reality (Future Crew, 1993). Every effect twice: as a 320x200,
256-colour 1993 version and as a path-traced 2026 version. Three acts, 31 chapters,
12:18 with music.

RUN
  Double-click Evolution.exe. The show starts in a window; F11 toggles fullscreen.
  Fullscreen from the start:  Evolution.exe --fullscreen --width 1920 --height 1080

KEYS
  F11 fullscreen   C CRT look   H chapter titles and cards   M mute   Esc quit

NEEDS
  Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and a graphics card with Vulkan hardware ray tracing
  (NVIDIA RTX 20 series or newer, AMD RX 6000 series or newer) with a current driver.
  The window aims at 60 fps and lowers its internal resolution where it must;
  Evolution.exe --target-fps 30 keeps a sharper picture at 30 fps.

SPEED CHECK
  Evolution.exe --bench --width 1280 --height 720
  prints one line per chapter with its fps.
